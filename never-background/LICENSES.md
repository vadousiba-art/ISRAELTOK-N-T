# Third-party assets and licenses

Everything here is free to use commercially and carries no watermark. None of these
files are committed: `./fetch-assets.sh` downloads them, because the Mixkit
licenses don't allow redistributing the raw items on their own. The license texts
are in `licenses/`.

## Everything else

The characters, manga art, typography layout, light leaks, dust, film grain,
synthesized drums and electronic pulse, and the composition and mix are original to
this project. The font is Inter (SIL Open Font License); the YORU wordmark is YORU's
own; the app screens are real captures of YORU (fictional demo accounts on a local
copy, labelled on screen).
