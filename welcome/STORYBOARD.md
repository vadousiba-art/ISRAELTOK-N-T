# YORU 夜 — "Enter the Night" (welcome film v1)

Original production. Every frame is drawn in code (Canvas 2D manga ink,
halftone, speed lines, the official wordmark) or is a **real screenshot of
the current build** captured from the app itself. Every sound is
synthesized. No stock footage, samples, paid services or imitation of any
existing opening or soundtrack.

## Formats

| Cut | Length | Sizes | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Full** ("Enter the Night") | 80 s | 1920×1080 (landscape) and 1080×1920 (portrait) | New accounts once; anyone on replay; the public landing page |
| **Short** ("Tonight on YORU") | 26 s | same two sizes | Existing members when a new welcome version ships |

Picture and sound share one cue sheet (`cues.json`, 120 BPM, one beat =
0.5 s, one bar = 2 s), so cuts and impacts land on the music.

On-screen copy is burned in English. Every line is also a caption cue whose
text comes from the app's translation files, so the captions follow the
member's language (16 languages) without re-rendering.

## Visual language

Black, charcoal, paper white, one crimson (#e5102f, the brand red). Manga
devices are used as punctuation, not wallpaper: an ink stroke reveals, a
panel border frames, speed lines carry a cut, halftone marks depth. Product
screens are shown large, sharp and readable; effects stay at the edges.
