# YORU Pro Universe (catalog v1)

The full YORU Pro customization universe from the Pro brief: 20 systems with
20 options each (400 items), a storefront, a membership hub, a Preview Lab
and the working tools behind the functional systems. **Purchases are not
open:** no payment provider is connected and the price (intended **CAD $5.99 a
month**) is not confirmed. Pro still comes only from admin complimentary grants.

### Testers

Members given complimentary Pro by an admin (Admin → Pro → Grant Pro) are
testers: they can use **every** cosmetic and tool in the catalog, including
the items normally unlocked by milestones. The 20 milestones themselves stay
earned-only, because showing one claims an achievement. When paid Pro opens,
paying members get the Pro items and earn the milestone ones as usual.
Experimental items still need the Preview Lab switch.

## 5. Migration 0009 and policies

Additive and safe to re-run. New tables: `pro_catalog`, `pro_equips`,
`pro_milestones`, `pro_wishlist`, `pro_settings`, `pro_drafts`,
`pro_scheduler`, `pro_profile_views`, `pro_profile_view_seen`. New columns
`collections.template` and `collections.sort` (defaulted). Three indexes on
existing tables (comments by author, reactions by user, bookmarks by post).

- RLS on every new table. Members can only **read their own** rows (the
  catalog is readable by members); no member can insert, update or delete
  any of them directly. Every write goes through a checked function.
- `pro_scheduler` and the visit dedupe table have no policy: server only.
- Ownership is decided by `pro_owns()` on the server (free, active Pro
  entitlement, or an earned milestone), not callable by members. Equipping a
  locked item, a wrong slot or an experimental item without the Lab switch is
  refused. Effective items (`pro_effective`) drop to the default while Pro is
  inactive; stored choices are kept and return on renewal. Nothing is deleted
  on expiry.
- Members can't create entitlements or milestones, run the publisher, or
  read another member's drafts, wishlist, settings or visit counts.
- Visits: one count per visitor per profile per day, keyed by a one-way hash
  that is unreadable and pruned after two days; no function returns viewers.
- Analytics read only the caller's posts and other members' activity on
  them; your own reactions and comments don't count.
- Scheduled posts: `pro_publish_due()` publishes through `create_post()` as
  the author, so mutes, bans, lockdown, slow mode, rate limits, media
  ownership and poll rules apply. 3 attempts, 5 minutes apart, then "failed"
  with the reason. pg_cron runs it every minute where the extension exists;
  scheduling is refused while no scheduler has run in 15 minutes.
- `profile_lite` gains `look` (effective ring, nameplate, badge);
  `get_profile_identity` gains `look` and `timeline`; `collection_json` gains
  `template` and `sort`. Signatures unchanged.

## Staff cosmetics (migration 0010)

Sixteen cosmetics that identify real YORU staff. They are separate from the 400-item catalog (`src/lib/pro/catalog/staff.ts`, `STAFF`), are never sold, earned or comp-granted, and never appear in the storefront for members.

| Set | Who | Items |
|---|---|---|
| Founder's Throne (王座) | role `owner` only | Founder's Eclipse ring, Throne of Night banner, Founder nameplate, Founder's Seal badge, Founder's Night theme, Founder's Aura effect, Founder's Crown reaction, Founder's Hall chat |
| The Night Watch (夜警) | role `admin` (and the owner) | Sentinel ring, The Night Watch banner, YORU Staff nameplate, Staff Shield badge, Watchtower theme, Watch Scan effect, Sentinel Pulse reaction, Watchtower Channel chat |

- Unlock values `staff:owner` and `staff:admin`. `pro_staff_allows()` reads the live `profiles.role` (and refuses banned accounts), so ownership follows the role: demote someone and their staff look falls back to the default on the next read. The stored choice is kept with `active: false`.
- Testers (complimentary Pro) do **not** get staff items; the staff rule runs before the tester rule in `pro_owns`.
- Equipping a staff item without the role fails with `FORBIDDEN|That item is reserved for YORU staff.`
- `get_my_pro()` returns `role`, which the shop uses to show a "Founder & staff" filter to staff only.
- Migration 0010 is additive: it widens the `pro_catalog.unlock` check, inserts 16 rows, and replaces `pro_owns`, `pro_check_equip` and `get_my_pro` with the same signatures.
- The Terms (section 9) forbid impersonating staff; the Trust Center explains that staff cosmetics are role-checked.

### Personal staff sets (migration 0012)

A set can also belong to one named member of staff. The unlock is `staff:member:<profile id>`: only that account can wear it, and only while its role is `owner` or `admin` (banned accounts never). Nobody else can, not other admins and not the founder, because a personal set says who someone is. These items live in `STAFF_MEMBER` (`STAFF_ROLE` is the role-based sixteen; `STAFF` is both) and are generated into migration 0012 by `npm run pro:catalog`.
