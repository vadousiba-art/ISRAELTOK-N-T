## Full description

YORU (夜) — THE INTERNET AFTER DARK. 夜は、まだ終わらない。

YORU is a free social network for anime, manga, music, Vocaloid, gaming and art fans. Post your takes, share stories, chat with your people and level up every night.

★ FEED & STORIES
• Post text, photos, GIFs, videos and polls
• 24-hour stories with a viewer
• Like, react, comment, repost and quote
• ||Spoilers|| stay blurred until tapped
• Double-tap a picture to like it
• Tonight's picks: posts featured by the community

★ FIND YOUR PEOPLE
• Explore Anime, Manga, Music, Vocaloid, Gaming, Art, Memes and Random
• Trending tags, search and member profiles
• Realtime group chats and DMs with replies, reactions and media

★ LEVEL UP AFTER DARK
• Earn XP for posting, commenting and getting likes
• Nightly check-ins and streaks
• Climb from 新人 Newcomer to 夜の王 Night Sovereign
• Level and streak leaderboards (opt-in)

★ MAKE IT YOURS
• Slide to recolor the entire app: YORU red, sakura, violet, ice, jade, gold or any color you want
• Ambient stars, sakura petals, rain or fireflies
• "Currently watching / reading / listening / playing" on your profile
• Pin your best post

★ THE AI MENACE
• An opt-in AI comedian that roasts posts, rates takes and joins the group chat
• Only roasts people who turn it on, at the intensity they choose

★ SAFE AND PRIVATE
• Posts are visible to members only, not the public web
• Report and block from any post, comment, story, message or profile
• Community moderation tools
• No ads, no selling your data, no tracking
• Delete your account any time in Settings

Free. 16+. Find your people. Your next conversation starts here.

## Message to the reviewers

YORU is a members-only social network for anime/manga/Vocaloid fans. The Android app opens the live service at yoru-social.vercel.app. Sign-up is open and free (16+). Moderation: report and block on all content, admin review, mutes, bans and a word filter. Account deletion is in Settings → Account and at https://yoru-social.vercel.app/delete-account.
